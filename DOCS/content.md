## 1.3 Why Choose Us (Detailed & Extended Breakdown)

At Adiyogi International, we bridge the gap between Indian agricultural abundance and international market demand. Below is a detailed breakdown of our foundational strengths and strategic advantages:

### 1. APEDA Registered (Agricultural and Processed Food Products Export Development Authority)
* **Description:** As an official APEDA-registered exporter, Adiyogi International adheres strictly to government-mandated quality, hygiene, and phytosanitary standards. This certification serves as a seal of quality assurance, confirming that all our agricultural products—from spices to processed foods—meet international export criteria and comply with global food safety regulations.

### 2. IEC Registered (Import Export Code)
* **Description:** Issued by the Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT), Government of India, our active IEC registration empowers us to conduct legitimate, frictionless international trade operations. It guarantees complete regulatory compliance, smooth customs clearance at both origin and destination ports, and legally sound cross-border financial transactions.

### 3. FSSAI Registered (Food Safety and Standards Authority of India)
* **Description:** Food safety is non-negotiable. Our FSSAI registration confirms that our sourcing, handling, storage, and packaging protocols conform to India's premier food safety benchmarks. Buyers can rest assured that every consignment is processed under strict hygienic conditions, ensuring non-contaminated, safe, and consumable products.

### 4. MSME Registered (Ministry of Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises)
* **Description:** Recognized under the Government of India's MSME framework, Adiyogi International combines operational agility with institutional credibility. This registration reflects our ethical business practices, strong governance, financial accountability, and commitment to driving economic growth within the agricultural sector.

### 5. Reliable Sourcing Network
* **Description:** Gujarat is India’s agricultural powerhouse. Leveraging our strategic location, we have established direct, longstanding relationships with local farmers, agricultural cooperatives, and verified APMC mandi networks. By bypassing unnecessary intermediaries, we maintain complete traceability, secure peak-harvest quality, and maintain supply continuity year-round.

### 6. Competitive & Transparent Pricing
* **Description:** We optimize our supply chain through lean direct-sourcing models and efficient logistics planning. This enables us to pass significant cost savings directly to our international buyers. We provide clear, transparent commercial offers—including FOB, CFR, and CIF pricing structures—with zero hidden charges.

### 7. Timely & Reliable Shipment
* **Description:** In the agro-export industry, timing is critical to maintaining product freshness and avoiding costly buyer downtime. Situated near major Indian maritime hubs like Mundra, Kandla, and Pipavav, we coordinate closely with leading ocean liners and freight forwarders to ensure swift container allocation, seamless customs clearance, and on-time vessel departures.

### 8. Flexible & Customized Packaging Options
* **Description:** We understand that packaging requirements vary according to country regulations, client branding, and product type. We offer highly customizable packaging solutions—ranging from bulk PP bags, jute bags, multi-layer paper bags, and vacuum bags to retail-ready private labeling (OEM). Our packaging preserves product aroma, extends shelf life, and prevents moisture ingress during sea transport.

### 9. Customer-First Business Approach
* **Description:** Our business model revolves around long-term relationship building rather than one-time transactions. We assign dedicated trade specialists to every account, offering transparent order updates, quick issue resolution, pre-shipment inspection reports, multi-currency trade support, and reliable post-delivery assistance.

## 3.3 The Adiyogi B2B Export Process (How We Work)

```
[ Step 1: Inquiry Received ] ➔ [ Step 2: Spec & Price Quote ] ➔ [ Step 3: Sample Approval ]
                                                                             │
[ Step 6: Dispatch & Port Delivery ] ◄─ [ Step 5: Quality Inspection ] ◄─ [ Step 4: Order & Contract ]
```

1. **Step 1: Inquiry & Requirement Analysis:** Submit your specifications, volume requirements, target port, and packaging choice.
2. **Step 2: Commercial Quotation:** We provide competitive FOB or CIF pricing based on current market dynamics.
3. **Step 3: Sample Dispatch:** Laboratory or physical samples are dispatched via express courier for client testing and verification.
4. **Step 4: Contract Execution:** Proforma Invoice (PI) and Sales Contract finalized with agreed payment terms (LC, TT, CAD).
5. **Step 5: Processing & Pre-Shipment Inspection:** Cleaning, sorting, grading, packaging, and third-party lab inspection (SGS/Geo-Chem).
6. **Step 6: Port Dispatch & Customs Clearance:** Container loading, ocean transport booking, phytosanitary clearance, and document dispatch via courier.
